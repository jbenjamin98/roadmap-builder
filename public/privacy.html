Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

100% Client-Side Architecture: Roadmap Builder Studio does not have a backend server, database, or analytics tracking. All roadmap creation, editing, and exports occur entirely within your web browser.

1. Overview

Roadmap Builder Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a client-side visual roadmap designer hosted on GitHub Pages at roadmap-builder.jacob-benjamin.com. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your data.

2. Your Files

Roadmaps you export, download, or save are written to your own device. In the optional desktop app, roadmaps are saved as plain .roadmap.json files in a folder on your computer (along with a local version history), and you choose where that folder is. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the app does not connect to any account or cloud service.

3. Browser Local Storage & IndexedDB

In the web version, the app saves a temporary draft in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). This draft remains strictly on your local device and is never transmitted over the internet.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Roadmap Builder Studio does not use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party telemetry scripts.
