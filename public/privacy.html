Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

100% Client-Side Architecture: Roadmap Builder Studio does not have a backend server, database, or analytics tracking. All roadmap creation, editing, and exports occur entirely within your web browser.

1. Overview

Roadmap Builder Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a client-side visual roadmap designer hosted on GitHub Pages at roadmap-builder.jacob-benjamin.com. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your data.

2. Google User Data & Google Drive Integration

Roadmap Builder Studio offers optional integration with Google Drive so you can save, sync, and restore roadmap files to your personal Google account.

Permissions & Scope Requested

When you connect Google Drive, we request the following restricted scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Permits the app to create new files and view, edit, or delete only files that were created by or explicitly opened with Roadmap Builder Studio.
  • userinfo.profile & userinfo.email — Used solely to display your email and avatar in the app's backup menu to confirm which account is connected.

What We Access & What We Never Access

  • We DO NOT have access to your other Google Drive files: The drive.file scope strictly prevents the app from accessing, reading, or listing any documents, photos, or folders in your Google Drive other than those created by Roadmap Builder Studio.
  • No Third-Party or Intermediary Servers: Communication with Google Drive occurs via direct, encrypted HTTPS requests between your web browser and Google's official endpoints (https://www.googleapis.com). Your access token, credentials, and roadmap contents are never sent to any developer, server, proxy, or third-party analytics service.
  • No Data Retention on External Servers: We do not operate external databases, log servers, or data collection systems.

Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Roadmap Builder Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking Access

You can disconnect your Google Drive at any time by clicking "Disconnect" in the app's Backup menu, or by revoking permissions in your Google Account Security Settings.

3. Browser Local Storage & IndexedDB

As you work, the app saves a temporary draft in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). This draft remains strictly on your local device and is never transmitted over the internet unless you choose to sync it to your Google Drive or export it.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Roadmap Builder Studio does not use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party telemetry scripts.
